# Best SRMA / Umbrella Review Topics — Curated Register

This is the **curated shortlist**, not a dump of every automatically discovered candidate. Daily topic leads remain in AHID's `outcomes/` reports and screening exports. A topic appears here only after substantive review.

## Daily register

Add one dated file per assessment day:

`best_topics/YYYY/MM/YYYY-MM-DD.md`

Each entry should list:
- Final draft title and review type (RCT-SRMA, observational SRMA, mixed-design only if justified, or umbrella review).
- PICO/PECO and clearly defined primary outcomes.
- Search date, databases searched and reproducible search strings.
- Verified included/eligible study count, separated from raw retrieval and screened counts.
- Total participants only when extractable and checked; avoid double-counting overlapping cohorts.
- Study-level DOI/PMID and links.
- Existing systematic reviews and PROSPERO overlap, with registry identifiers/status/date when verified.
- Outcome/timepoint compatibility, expected heterogeneity and pooling plan.
- Risk-of-bias and certainty plan.
- Novelty gap, limitations, and publication-potential assessment with no guaranteed journal/impact factor.

## Promotion criteria

A topic may be marked **Shortlisted** only after:
1. The clinical question is specific and clinically meaningful.
2. Primary-study evidence is confirmed beyond raw PubMed hit counts.
3. A preliminary search for existing reviews and PROSPERO records is documented.
4. At least one meaningful, defensible novelty angle is identified.
5. Outcomes/designs appear sufficiently compatible for a feasible review, or a clear narrative/synthesis alternative is justified.
6. Major feasibility risks and missing full texts are disclosed.

## Status labels

- **Shortlisted** — evidence and novelty look sufficiently promising for protocol-level work; still requires full systematic searching and screening.
- **Promising—verification pending** — worth further checking, but a key evidence/overlap/outcome question remains.
- **Hold** — potentially useful, but currently blocked by overlap, sparse evidence, or unresolved methods.
- **Rejected** — a documented reason makes it a poor current choice.

Do not promote a topic solely because AHID assigns it a high heuristic score. No automated score is a validated publication-probability estimate.
