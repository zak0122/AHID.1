### Topic entry format

Every listed topic must contain:

| Field | What will be recorded |
|---|---|
| Rank / priority | High, medium, or reserve, with a reason |
| Final working title | Specific, clinically meaningful review question |
| Review design | RCT-only SRMA, observational SRMA, or umbrella review; mixed designs only with a justified plan |
| PICO / PECO | Population, intervention/exposure, comparator and outcomes |
| Primary outcomes | Definitions, measures and timepoints; whether comparable across studies |
| Evidence base | Verified primary-study count, separated into retrieved, screened, full-text assessed and eligible |
| Participants | Verified total only; disclose unavailable data and possible cohort overlap |
| Key studies | DOI, PMID and source links for the actual studies supporting feasibility |
| Existing reviews / PROSPERO | Search date, records checked, IDs and links; exact/close/partial overlap and differences |
| Poolability | Outcome compatibility, expected heterogeneity and proposed synthesis; no claim based only on search hits |
| Bias / certainty | Appropriate RoB tool and GRADE/certainty plan |
| Novelty | Specific gap versus existing reviews |
| Main limitations | Missing full texts, sparse evidence, indirectness, overlapping cohorts, etc. |
| Recommendation | Why this is worth pursuing and what must be checked next |

## What qualifies a topic for this shortlist?

A candidate is added only after the available evidence supports all of the following:

1. **A clear question:** clinically relevant PICO/PECO and a defined review type.
2. **Primary evidence exists:** primary studies are checked by DOI/PMID and source records. PubMed result counts alone never count as eligible studies.
3. **The evidence is potentially synthesizable:** outcomes, definitions, measures and timepoints are sufficiently compatible, or a defensible alternative synthesis is specified.
4. **Novelty has been checked:** existing systematic reviews and public PROSPERO records have been searched and meaningful differences are documented.
5. **Feasibility risks are visible:** likely heterogeneity, cohort overlap, missing full texts, small evidence base and risk-of-bias concerns are disclosed.
6. **The recommendation is honest:** no invented population totals, study counts, DOIs, registry details, impact factors or publication probabilities.

The first pass may rely on abstracts and registry records. In that case, the entry must say **preliminary** and list the required full-text checks. It must never claim final eligibility before full-text screening.

## What this document is — and is not

This file is the **curated shortlist and activity summary**, not the full systematic-search archive. AHID's `outcomes/` folder continues to hold raw daily automated reports and screening exports. `chatgpt_worklog/` retains detailed records of substantive ChatGPT analysis. You do not need to browse those folders for the daily shortlist.

A high AHID heuristic score is only a discovery signal. It is not proof of novelty, poolability, publication potential or eligibility. No topic is guaranteed acceptance or a particular journal tier.
